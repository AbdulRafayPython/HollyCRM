## Visa and Documents

Umrah visas are issued through the Nusuk platform. We submit the application on
your behalf once we have your documents.

We need a passport valid for at least six months from your date of travel, a
recent white-background photograph, and your completed booking form. Women under
45 travelling without a mahram must provide the group leader's details.

Visa processing normally takes three to five working days. During Ramadan and
the two weeks before Hajj it can take up to ten working days, so book early.

A Saudi tourist visa also permits Umrah. If you already hold a valid tourist
visa you do not need a separate Umrah visa.

## Airport Transfers

We arrange transfers from Jeddah and Madinah airports to your hotel.

Transfers are included free of charge in any package of five nights or more.
For shorter stays the charge is SAR 350 per car each way, seating up to four
passengers with luggage.

Groups of more than eight travellers are moved by coach. Coach transfers must be
booked at least 48 hours before arrival so we can confirm the vehicle.

Our driver meets you in the arrivals hall holding a Hollyland Travel sign. If
your flight is delayed, message us on WhatsApp and the pickup is rescheduled at
no extra cost.

## Transport Between Makkah and Madinah

The Haramain high-speed train takes about two and a half hours. We book tickets
on request and they are not included in hotel packages by default.

Private car transfer between the two cities is available as an alternative and
takes roughly four hours depending on traffic.

## Ziyarat Tours

Ziyarat visits to the historical sites are offered in both cities.

The Makkah tour covers Jabal al-Nour, Jabal Thawr, Mina, Muzdalifah and Arafat,
and takes about four hours. The Madinah tour covers Masjid Quba, Masjid Qiblatain,
the Seven Mosques and Mount Uhud, and takes about three hours.

Tours run in the morning and depart from your hotel lobby. They can be added at
any point during your stay, subject to availability.

## Payment

We accept bank transfer, cash at our office, and card payment over the phone.

A deposit of 30 percent confirms your booking. The balance is due 14 days before
departure. For bookings made within 14 days of travel, full payment is required
at the time of booking.

Payment plans are available for group bookings of ten people or more. Speak to
your booking agent about arranging one.

We do not accept payment through third parties or personal accounts. If someone
asks you to transfer money to an account not on our official invoice, contact us
before sending anything.

## Cancellation and Refunds

Cancellations more than 30 days before check-in are refunded in full, less any
visa fees already paid to the Saudi authorities.

Cancellations between 30 and 14 days before check-in are refunded at 50 percent.

Cancellations within 14 days of check-in are not refundable, because hotel
allocations are paid in full at that point and cannot be returned.

Date changes are treated as a new booking and are subject to availability and
the rate applying on the new dates.

If we cancel your booking for any reason, you receive a full refund including
visa fees.

## Hotel Check-in and Check-out

Standard check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 12 noon.

Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability on the day and cannot be
guaranteed in advance. Ask at reception on arrival.

Rooms are allocated by the hotel, not by us. If you need adjoining rooms or a
particular floor, tell us at the time of booking and we will pass the request on,
but the hotel decides.

Children under six stay free when sharing an existing bed. Children six and over
count as adults for room occupancy.

## Luggage

Most airlines allow 30kg checked baggage on flights to Saudi Arabia, but this
varies by carrier and ticket type. Check your airline's rules.

Zamzam water cannot be carried in hand luggage. It is sold in sealed five-litre
containers at the airport after security, and most airlines allow one container
per passenger in the hold.

Hotels hold luggage free of charge on your check-out day if your flight is later
that evening.

## Wheelchair and Accessibility

Wheelchairs are available for hire at both Harams and can be booked in advance
through us.

Electric wheelchair permits for the Haram are issued on site and cannot be
reserved ahead of time.

Tell us at the time of booking if anyone in your group has mobility needs, so we
can request a room close to the lift and on a lower floor.

## Office Hours and Contact

Our office is open Saturday to Thursday, 9am to 7pm Saudi time. We are closed on
Fridays.

WhatsApp messages are monitored during office hours. Messages sent outside those
hours are answered the next working morning.

For emergencies while you are travelling, use the emergency number printed on
your booking voucher. It is answered 24 hours a day.

## Group Bookings

Groups of ten or more get a dedicated coordinator who handles the whole booking
from visa to return transfer.

Group rooming lists must be confirmed 21 days before departure. Name changes
after that point may incur a hotel amendment fee.

One free place is offered for every twenty paying passengers on group bookings.
