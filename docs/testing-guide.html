Testing the WhatsApp AI sales agent

Eighteen checks covering how the agent replies, what it remembers, where it gets its prices, and who it hands a conversation to. Work top to bottom — later tests depend on setup done in earlier ones.

Before you start

Five things must be true or most tests below will fail for the wrong reason. Tick each one off.

The one thing that will confuse you When the agent hands a chat to a human, it pauses itself on that chat so it cannot talk over you. Any message you send after that gets no AI reply — this is correct, not a bug. To carry on testing in the same chat, flip the AI Assistant toggle at the top of the conversation back on.
01

How the agent replies

Run these in the built-in tester: AI agent → Workflow → Test workflow. Nothing is sent to WhatsApp, no chats are assigned and no leads move, so this is safe to run at any time. Type the message, press Run test.

A-01

It greets back properly

A greeting should get a warm, short reply — not a sales pitch.

Send exactly

Assalamu alaikum

Expected

  • Replies with a greeting back, in the same style (Wa alaikum assalam)
  • One or two lines only — no hotel list, no prices
  • Trace shows Understand → intent greeting
A-02

It answers a greeting mid-conversation

The original defect: the agent went silent on any greeting after the first message.

Send exactly

hello are you there?

Expected

  • A friendly reply — not silence and not "no options found"
  • If a question was outstanding, it repeats that one question gently
A-03

It quotes real prices

Prices must come from the inventory database, never invented by the AI.

Send exactly

5 star hotel in Makkah, 12-15 September, 5 people

Expected

  • Real hotel names with SAR prices and distance to the Haram
  • Every hotel named appears in Settings → Inventory — check one
  • Says how many rooms (e.g. 2 × Quad) — 5 people do not fit in one room
  • Ends with a question that moves the booking forward

Verify: open Settings → Inventory and confirm the quoted price matches a rate row for those dates. If it does not, that is a critical failure.

A-04

It asks instead of guessing

Missing dates or city must produce a question, never an assumed booking.

Send exactly

I need a hotel next month

Expected

  • Asks for the city and dates
  • Does not invent a date or pick a city
  • Trace shows Inventory search skipped, with the reason
A-05

It answers from your uploaded document

Policy questions should be answered from your own file, not from the model's general knowledge.

Send exactly

Do you arrange transport from Jeddah airport?
What is your cancellation policy?

Expected

  • Answers using details from the uploaded document — free for 5+ nights, SAR 350 per car otherwise
  • Cancellation answer matches the 30 / 14 day tiers in the file
  • Trace shows Knowledge base matched, listing the source
A-06

It admits when it does not know

The most important safety behaviour: no confident invented answers.

Send exactly

Do you offer travel insurance for over 70s?

Expected

  • Says it does not have the confirmed answer and a colleague will follow up
  • Does not make up a policy, a price or a provider name
  • Trace shows Knowledge base found nothing → goes to Route & assign
A-07

It never negotiates

Discount requests must reach a person, always.

Send exactly

Can I get a discount on that?

Expected

  • Says a colleague will be in touch — no discount offered, no counter-offer
  • Trace jumps straight to Route & assign, skipping inventory
02

Memory and knowing who is talking

These need real WhatsApp messages to the connected business number, because they test memory that builds up across a conversation. Send from your own phone and watch the chat in the Inbox.

Between tests If the AI stops replying, it has handed the chat to a human. Turn the AI Assistant toggle back on at the top of the conversation before continuing.
B-08

It remembers across messages

The agent must not re-ask something already answered.

Send these one at a time, waiting for each reply

I need a 5 star hotel
Makkah
12 to 15 September, 4 people

Expected

  • After the third message it quotes — it still remembers 5 star from the first message and Makkah from the second
  • It never asks for the city twice
  • Quoted hotels are all 5-star

Verify: open the Lead details panel on the right — Guests, Check-in, Check-out should all be filled in.

B-09

It handles a non-answer

"Don't know" should change the question, then fetch a human — not loop.

In a fresh chat, send

Hotel in Madinah from 20 September
dont know
not sure

Expected

  • First it asks for the check-out date
  • Second time it rephrases — asks how many nights instead
  • Third time it stops asking and passes you to a person
  • The same sentence is never sent twice
B-10

It knows who is speaking in a group

Group messages must show the sender's real name, not "Client".

  1. Add the business number to a WhatsApp group with at least two other people
  2. Have someone send a message mentioning the business number, or containing the word hotel

Expected

  • The message bubble in the Inbox is labelled with the sender's name
  • The header shows the real participant count, not 0 participants
  • If the reply addresses someone, it uses their name

Note: in groups the agent stays quiet unless it is mentioned or a trigger word appears. Silence on casual group chatter is correct.

B-11

Two people in one group stay separate

Different people asking in the same group must not merge into one enquiry.

  1. Person 1 sends: hotel in Makkah 12-15 September, 4 people
  2. Person 2 sends: hotel in Madinah 20-25 September, 2 people
  3. Person 1 sends: can you do 6 people instead?

Expected

  • Person 1's follow-up is quoted for Makkah, not Madinah
  • The Pipeline shows two separate leads from that one group
  • Neither person's dates leak into the other's quote

Verify: open Pipeline and confirm two distinct leads exist with different cities.

03

Handing over to a salesperson

This is the part your team will feel day to day: when the AI gives up, does a real person actually get the chat?

C-12

It assigns to an online agent

A handover must land on a named person, not an anonymous queue.

  1. Confirm you are set to Available (green dot on your avatar)
  2. From your phone, send: Can I speak to someone about a discount?

Expected

  • Customer gets a message saying a colleague will be in touch shortly
  • The chat is now assigned to you — your name replaces "Unassigned"
  • The AI Assistant toggle has switched itself off
  • The Notes tab records who took it and why

Verify: the note should read something like "Bot handed off to a human. Reason: requested_human. Assigned to [your name]."

C-13

It tells the truth when nobody is available

The agent must not promise someone is coming when nobody is there.

  1. Set yourself to Away (avatar → Away). Make sure no colleague is online.
  2. From a different phone number, send: I want to complain about my booking

Expected

  • Customer gets the out-of-hours wording — not "someone will contact you shortly"
  • The chat stays Unassigned
  • The note says nobody was available

Why this matters: C-12 and C-13 are the same message with opposite outcomes, decided purely by whether a human is at their desk. Set yourself back to Available afterwards.

C-14

It routes by country

Customers should reach the desk that handles their market.

  1. Settings → Routing & team → create a region named Pakistan desk with code 92, and assign only yourself
  2. Turn off "Allow assignment outside the region"
  3. From a +92 number, trigger a handover (I need to speak to a manager)

Expected

  • Assigned to you, and the note names the region
  • Repeat from a non-+92 number with yourself removed from the default region → nobody is assigned and the fallback message is sent
04

Your own rules

Rules are if/else you write yourself. They run before the built-in flow decides anything, so they always win.

D-15

A rule overrides the AI

Prove the workspace can change behaviour without a developer.

  1. Go to AI agent → Rules → Add rule
  2. Name it Big enquiries to me
  3. Condition: Budget per night · is more than · 50000
  4. Action: Assign to a specific person → yourself
  5. Save, then in the Workflow tester send: Makkah 12-15 September, 4 people, budget 80000 SAR per night

Expected

  • Trace shows Your rules matched, naming your rule
  • It goes to handover instead of quoting
  • Back on the Rules page, the rule shows matched 1×

Then check the opposite: send the same message without the budget. The rule must not fire, and a normal quote should come back.

05

The workflow editor

The visual map of everything above, at AI agent → Workflow.

E-16

Nodes move and stay moved

The layout is saved for the whole team, not just this browser.

  1. Drag two or three nodes to new positions
  2. Wait for Saving… to appear and disappear
  3. Reload the page

Expected

  • Nodes are exactly where you left them
  • Tidy up restores the original arrangement
E-17

Each step shows what it received and produced

You can follow one message through the whole flow.

  1. Run the test message from A-03
  2. Close the test panel, then click the Inventory search node

Expected

  • A window opens in the middle with three columns
  • Input shows what Understand extracted — Makkah, the dates, 5 people
  • Output shows how many hotels were found, and where it goes next
  • Clicking Understand shows the same data as its output
E-18

Switching a step off actually changes behaviour

The toggles must control the live agent, not just the picture.

  1. Switch off the Knowledge base node using the toggle on the node
  2. Run What is your cancellation policy? in the tester
  3. Switch it back on and run the same message again

Expected

  • Off → goes straight to a human, no document answer
  • On → answers from the document again
  • The node and its connectors dim while switched off
06

What counts as a failure

Not every surprise is a bug. These are the ones that matter, ranked.

SeverityWhat you would seeWhy it matters
Critical A quoted price that is not in Inventory The agency could be held to a price it cannot honour
Critical An invented policy, visa rule or refund term A customer acts on it before anyone sees the message
High "Someone will contact you" when nobody was assigned A promise the team has no way to keep
High Two people's requirements merged into one quote Both customers get the wrong answer
Medium The same question asked twice word for word Reads as broken; customers stop replying
Medium Silence on a greeting mid-conversation Looks like the number is dead
Low Wording that is stiff or slightly off-tone Adjustable in Settings without a code change
When reporting a problem Include the exact message you sent, the reply you got, and — if it happened in the Workflow tester — which step in the trace looked wrong. That trace turns "it said something odd" into something that can be fixed.
